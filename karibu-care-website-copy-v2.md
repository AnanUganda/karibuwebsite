## You know this afternoon.

Your guest checks out at 11 and the next one arrives at 2. You're stuck in a meeting in Nakasero, and your cleaner hasn't replied since morning. Did they come? Did they clear the shower drain? Is there tissue in the bathroom? You won't find out until the guest tells you, in a review.

### 4. What you get

## What lands on your phone after every turnover.

**Room-by-room photos.** You see the bed made, the bathroom done and the kitchen reset. You get proof, not a promise.

**A supplies note.** If tissue, soap or coffee is running low, you hear it from us, not from your guest.

**A damage report.** Anything broken, leaking or missing is photographed the same day, so you have evidence if you need to make a claim.

Behind the photos is the full clean: fresh linen on the bed, towels replaced, the bathroom scrubbed right down to the drain, the kitchen and dishes done, floors mopped (including the red dust by the door) and bins emptied. We also leave a welcome card for your guest, in your name.

### 7. FAQ

## Questions hosts ask us.

**Can I trust your cleaners with my unit?**
Every cleaner is vetted with a national ID, an LC1 letter, references and a police certificate of good conduct. You get their name and photo before each job, and our own numbers are on every booking.

**How do you get in?**
We agree this at setup. It could be a key held by our team lead, a lockbox, or your caretaker or guard. Either way, you always know who has access.

**What about same-day bookings?**
New bookings show up on your calendar link as they come in. For a same-day turnover, message us and we'll confirm within [RESPONSE TIME] whether we can make it.

**What if something gets damaged?**
If a guest breaks something, our damage report gives you photos for your claim. If we cause damage, tell us within 24 hours and we'll handle it under our written breakages policy.

**Do you wash the linen?**
We remake the bed with your fresh linen. Off-site laundry is available as an add-on.

**How do I pay?**
You get one invoice at the end of the month, payable by mobile money.

**Which areas do you cover?**

## ABOUT

### Our name

# Karibu means "you are welcome."

In East Africa, *karibu* is what you say when someone arrives at your door. We named our company after it because a host's business depends on that moment. A guest opens the door, and the place either says welcome or it doesn't. Our job is to make sure it does.
