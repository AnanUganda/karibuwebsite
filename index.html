Questions hosts ask us.

Can I trust your cleaners with my unit?

Every cleaner is vetted with a national ID, an LC1 letter, references and a police certificate of good conduct. You get their name and photo before each job, and our own numbers are on every booking.

How do you get in?

We agree this at setup. It could be a key held by our team lead, a lockbox, or your caretaker or guard. Either way, you always know who has access.

What about same-day bookings?

New bookings show up on your calendar link as they come in. For a same-day turnover, message us and we'll confirm within [RESPONSE TIME] whether we can make it.

What if something gets damaged?

If a guest breaks something, our damage report gives you photos for your claim. If we cause damage, tell us within 24 hours and we'll handle it under our written breakages policy.

Do you wash the linen?

We remake the bed with your fresh linen. Off-site laundry is available as an add-on.

How do I pay?

You get one invoice at the end of the month, payable by mobile money.
